Deliberately Rejected Decision

Rejected Choice

Automated Pipeline Values & Rep Rankings:

We considered synthesizing total deal pipeline values and performance stack rankings across reps.

Why Rejected: The dataset contains no authoritative CRM deal table, pipeline stages, or account lifetime values. Fabricating pipeline currency figures from conversational interest would create dangerously misleading revenue forecasts.

Trade-off Accepted: The workspace focuses on verifiable conversation signals and stated next steps without speculative financial figures.
